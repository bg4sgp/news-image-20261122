One image that sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026: a newspaper front page led by the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya — nearly 200 countries leaving Türkiye without a deal after a record 48 hours of overtime, the EU refusing a text with no fossil-fuel phase-out — with panels on the suspected Ukrainian sea-drone strike on the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, the Russian drone strike on flats near Kyiv that left 14 dead or missing, Iran's strikes on a Bahraini plant and tankers near Hormuz, the West Bank settler arson raids condemned by Eisenkot, the Democrats' biggest US House majority since 1992, and Verstappen's Las Vegas win alongside Liverpool's 4–0 rout of Manchester United — plus an "also today" ticker (the capture of cartel kingpin 'Chapo Isidro', the Nigeria boat capsize, the Indonesia factory fire, the EU–Indonesia trade deal, the US probe of Nvidia's chip sales to China, and India's 720/9 against New Zealand). The page is drawn entirely in code by `make_image.py` (Python + Pillow); `main.py` validates the image against the bounty's gate and writes it to /output/entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

To redraw the page from scratch: `python3 make_image.py`.
